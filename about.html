About MooduTools

A local, browser-AI vibe & story art generator

Our Mission

MooduTools was created around a simple idea: your feelings deserve to move. Instead of describing a mood with words alone, you type how you feel and an on-device AI immediately writes a unique, animated HTML/CSS piece of art — and a tiny poetic micro-story to go with it.

Everything runs inside your browser (Chrome with Gemini Nano). Your mood never leaves your machine, and no account or app is ever required.

What Makes Us Different

Every experience on MooduTools is built on three principles:

  • 100% On-Device — The AI generates right on your hardware. What you type or create is never transmitted to a server.
  • Free Forever — No paywalls, no premium tiers, no hidden charges. Every feature is available to everyone.
  • No Installation — Open the page, tap a vibe, and watch living art appear. No downloads, no sign-ups.

Who We Are

MooduTools is an independent web project by developers and storytellers who believe generative creativity should be approachable, fast, and private. We love the gap between "an emotion" and "a moving picture" — and we built MooduTools to close it.
