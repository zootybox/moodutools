Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

TL;DR — 100% On-Device Processing, Zero Server Logs

MooduTools generates your vibe art entirely inside your browser. The in-browser AI (Gemini Nano) runs on your own device, so the moods you type and the art you create are never sent to our servers. We collect no personal data, keep no server logs of your creations, and have nothing to leak.

What We Do Not Collect

We do not store, transmit, or log:

  • Your mood text or prompts
  • Generated art, micro-stories, or downloads
  • Your name, email, or any account details (none are required)
  • Any content you produce with the tool

The In-Browser AI (Gemini Nano)

Generation is powered by Chrome's built-in Gemini Nano, which executes locally on your device. When you create a vibe, your prompt goes from the page to your own browser's AI model on the same machine — never across the network to us. Because nothing is transmitted, no third party (including us) sees what you create.

Anonymous Site Statistics

Like most websites, we may use standard, privacy-conscious analytics to measure aggregate page visits. These anonymous counts help us improve the site and do not identify you or capture your creations. To avoid it entirely, you can enable your browser's Do-Not-Track or use a private window.

Advertising

To keep MooduTools free, our pages may display advertisements (for example, Google AdSense). Ad networks may use cookies to serve relevant ads across the web. These relate to your browser's advertising profile, not to your moods or creations. You can opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking your creations. Cookies on the site are limited to those set by our advertising and analytics partners described above. You can clear or block cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Third-Party Links

Our pages may link to external sites or applications. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. We encourage you to review their policies before engaging with them.

Data Security

Because your mood and art never leave your device, the strongest guarantee we can give is simple: there is no stored data to be exposed. Any settings your browser keeps locally, such as cached pages, can be cleared through your browser's privacy settings.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has interacted with the site, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Contact us through our Contact page.
