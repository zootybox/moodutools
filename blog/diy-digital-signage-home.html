DIY Digital Signage for Your Home Using Old Phones

By MooduToolsJanuary 15, 20258 min read

Almost everyone has at least one old smartphone sitting in a drawer, replaced by a newer model but still perfectly functional. Instead of letting that device collect dust, you can transform it into a permanent digital sign for your home. A dedicated LED display in your kitchen, workshop, home office, or entryway adds both functionality and a modern aesthetic to your living space.

Why Repurpose Old Phones as Home Signs

Old smartphones are ideal for home digital signage because they already have everything needed: a bright screen, a web browser, Wi-Fi connectivity, and a battery that can supplement wired power. Unlike dedicated digital signage hardware that can cost hundreds of dollars, repurposing an old phone costs nothing beyond a charging cable you likely already own.

The environmental benefit is worth mentioning too. Electronic waste is a growing problem, and giving an old phone a second life as a useful home device keeps it out of a landfill for years longer. It is a genuinely practical form of recycling.

Best Locations for Home Digital Signs

Kitchen

The kitchen is one of the best rooms for a permanent LED display. Mount the phone on a small stand on the counter or attach it to the side of a cabinet at eye level. Use it to display the current recipe step while cooking, a grocery list that scrolls continuously so you can check it at a glance, or motivational messages to brighten your morning routine.

A practical kitchen application is displaying meal prep reminders: "Marinate chicken by 3PM" or "Bread dough — punch down at 5PM." These time-sensitive reminders are easy to miss on a paper note but hard to ignore when they are scrolling in bright text on your counter.

Home Office

In a home office, a phone LED sign serves as a dynamic reminder board. Display your top priority for the day, a motivational quote, or your current Pomodoro session status. Some people use it to display their daily schedule in a slow scroll, providing a constant awareness of upcoming meetings and deadlines without needing to check a calendar app.

Workshop or Garage

Workshops benefit from LED signs displaying safety reminders, project measurements, or step-by-step instructions. When your hands are covered in sawdust or grease, you cannot easily check your phone for the next step in a project. A dedicated display with large, scrolling text provides hands-free reference that you can read from across the room.

Entryway

A phone near the front door displaying daily reminders creates a last-check point before leaving the house. Messages like "Keys? Wallet? Badge? Lunch?" help prevent the frustrating experience of arriving at work and realizing you forgot something essential. Update the message for special days: "Do not forget — dentist at 2PM."

Kids Rooms

Help children develop routines with a scrolling display showing their morning or bedtime checklist. "Brush teeth — Pack bag — Check homework" scrolling on a small phone creates a visual routine guide that kids can follow independently. The novelty of the glowing text makes routine tasks feel slightly more exciting.

Setting Up a Permanent Display

Power

A permanent display needs permanent power. Connect the phone to a charger that will remain plugged in continuously. Most modern phones handle continuous charging well, as they stop drawing power once the battery is full. Position the charging cable along the wall or behind furniture for a clean look.

Mounting Options

Small phone stands are the simplest option and allow you to move the display easily. For a more permanent installation, adhesive phone mounts attach to walls, cabinets, or shelves without leaving holes. Magnetic mounts work well if you attach a magnetic plate to the back of the phone. For the cleanest look, some people 3D print custom holders or use small floating shelves.

Screen Burn-In Prevention

Displaying the same content continuously for months can cause screen burn-in on some display types, particularly OLED screens. The scrolling animation of an LED sign naturally mitigates this risk because no single element stays in the same position for long. However, for extra protection, periodically change the text color or background color to shift which pixels are most active.

Auto-Start After Power Loss

If power is interrupted and restored, you want the LED sign to restart automatically. Most phones will boot up when connected to power. Set the phone to skip the lock screen and open the browser automatically on startup. Bookmark the MooduTools LED Sign Maker as the browser homepage so it loads automatically.

Creative Home Sign Ideas

Beyond practical reminders, home LED signs can serve purely aesthetic purposes. A slow-scrolling inspirational quote in your living room adds a modern art element. A display showing the current date and a seasonal greeting near the front door welcomes visitors. In a game room or entertainment space, scrolling text with the name of the game being played or the movie being watched adds ambiance.

Some people create rotating messages that change with the season or holiday: festive greetings in December, spooky messages in October, romantic quotes in February. The ability to update the message instantly means your home decor can evolve as often as your mood.
